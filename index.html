GENERATION & CAPACITY GUIDE

Know what is stored and what is generated

VERIFIED 24 AUGUST 2026

CAPACITY ≠ DAILY QUOTA

Capacity is how many notebooks and sources you can keep. It does not reset each day.

GENERATION QUOTA

This is how many chats or Studio outputs you can create. Daily quotas reset after 24 hours; monthly quotas after 30 days.
